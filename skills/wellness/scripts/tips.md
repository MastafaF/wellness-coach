# Wellness Tips
# Format: each tip is a single line starting with the category tag
# Categories: breathing, stretch-home, stretch-office, presence, eyes

## breathing

[breathing] Box breathing reset: inhale 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Repeat 4 cycles. Activates your parasympathetic system in under 2 minutes.
[breathing] Physiological sigh: double inhale through the nose (sniff, then sniff again to fill lungs fully), then one long exhale. Do 3 times. Fastest known way to lower heart rate.
[breathing] 4-7-8 breath: inhale 4 counts, hold 7, exhale slowly for 8. Three rounds resets cortisol. Good before a hard problem.
[breathing] Belly breath check: place one hand on your chest, one on your belly. Take 5 breaths where only the belly hand moves. You've been chest-breathing — this fixes it.

## stretch-home

[stretch-home] Cat-cow on the mat: hands and knees, arch your back on inhale (cow), round it on exhale (cat). 10 slow cycles. Undoes an hour of desk posture.
[stretch-home] Low lunge hip flexor: step one foot forward into a lunge, drop the back knee to the mat. Hold 45 seconds each side. Counters all that sitting.
[stretch-home] Supine spinal twist: lie on your back, pull one knee to your chest and let it fall across your body. Arms wide, breathe into the side body. 30 seconds each side.
[stretch-home] Downward dog hold: from hands and knees, tuck toes and lift hips. Pedal the heels slowly for 10 reps, then hold still for 5 breaths. Full-body reset.

## stretch-office

[stretch-office] Seated neck release: sit tall, drop your right ear toward your right shoulder. Left hand rests in your lap (no pulling). Hold 30 seconds. Switch sides. Melts tension.
[stretch-office] Thoracic opener: fingers interlaced behind your head, elbows wide. Lean back gently over the top of your chair. 3 slow breaths. Reverses forward-head posture.
[stretch-office] Seated figure-four: cross one ankle over the opposite knee. Sit tall and lean forward slightly. Hold 30-45 seconds each side. Hip flexor relief without leaving your chair.
[stretch-office] Wrist and forearm reset: extend one arm, palm up. With the other hand, gently pull fingers down toward the floor. Hold 20 seconds. Switch. Your tendons will thank you.

## presence

[presence] 5-4-3-2-1 ground: name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste. Pulls you out of your head and into the room in 60 seconds.
[presence] One-minute body scan: close your eyes. Slowly move attention from the top of your head to your feet. Notice tension without trying to fix it. Just observe.
[presence] Intention reset: before writing the next line of code, take one breath and state (silently) what you're about to do. Reduces scatter-brain context-switching.
[presence] Gratitude micro-pause: think of one specific thing that went well in your session today — not general, one concrete moment. 30 seconds of this shifts cognitive load.

## eyes

[eyes] 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Do it now — pick a spot across the room and let your eyes relax their focus.
[eyes] Palming: rub your palms together until warm, then cup them gently over your closed eyes. Hold for 30 seconds. The darkness and warmth reduce ciliary muscle tension.
[eyes] Blink reset: stare at the screen for a moment and notice how infrequently you blink. Now take 10 deliberate, slow blinks. Screen work drops blink rate by 60% — dry eyes accumulate.
